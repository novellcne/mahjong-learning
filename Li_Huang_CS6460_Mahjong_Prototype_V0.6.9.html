Dots / 饼子（常称筒子）

Visual strategy: count the circles.
识别方法：数牌面上的圆圈。

For most Dot tiles, the number of circles directly tells you the tile value. This makes Dots a useful starting point for a complete beginner.

MCR 中文称“饼子牌”，日常也常称“筒子”。大多数这类牌可以直接通过圆圈数量判断数字，因此适合作为初学者的第一组牌。

Bamboo / 条子

Visual strategy: count the bamboo-like marks.
识别方法：多数情况下数竹节/条形图案。

Most Bamboo tiles use repeated bamboo-like marks. 1 Bamboo is the special case: it uses a distinctive bird-like design rather than a single stick.

大多数条子牌可通过条形图案数量识别；一条是特殊牌面，通常是明显的鸟形图案。

Characters / 万字牌

Visual strategy: numeral on top + 万/萬 suit marker below.
识别方法：上部看中文数字，下部用“万/萬”确认花色。

You do not need general Chinese literacy. Learn only the nine numerals used on Character tiles, then recognize the recurring 万/萬 marker.

不需要掌握完整中文，只需认识一到九的数字，并把下方反复出现的“万/萬”作为花色标志。

Visual strategy: learn direct symbol ↔ meaning pairs.
识别方法：直接建立“符号 ↔ 英文含义”的对应关系。

Chow pattern → Chi call

chī

A Chow is three sequentially-numbered tiles of the same suit. In MCR, when the player to your left discards the needed tile, you may claim it by calling “Chi”.

顺子由同一花色的三张连续序数牌组成；在国标麻将中，上家打出所需牌时，可报“吃”来完成顺子。

Say / 说： 吃 · chī · Chi
Do / 做： Expose your two tiles and combine them with the player-to-your-left’s discard to make the Chow.

Hu / Mahjong / Win
和
hú

“Hu” is the declaration used when claiming a legal winning Mahjong hand. A common normal structure uses four sets and one pair; full MCR scoring requirements are intentionally deferred.

“和”是宣布合法和牌时使用的规范口令。常见基本结构为四组牌加一对；完整国标麻将计分要求暂不展开。

Say / 说： 和 · hú · Hu
Do / 做： Declare the win first, then arrange and reveal the completed hand for confirmation; full scoring is deferred.
先报“和”，再整理并亮明完成的手牌供确认；完整计分留待后续。

Make a Decision

Learning goal / 学习目标Transfer pattern knowledge into legal actions across changing contexts.

Can you claim this discard?

Complete all 3 scenarios to finish this step. / 完成 3 个情境后，本阶段才算完成。

Why multiple scenarios?

The rule stays stable while the situation changes. This checks whether the learner can transfer the mapping instead of remembering one example.
